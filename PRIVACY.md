# Privacy Policy — Resume ATS Scorer

_Last updated: 30 September 2026_

Resume ATS Scorer is a free, open-source portfolio project by Aakash Kumar Singh
(source code: https://github.com/aakashcse/ai-resume-ats).

## What we collect
When you choose **Continue with Google**, Google shares the following with the app:
- your name
- your email address
- your profile picture URL
- your Google account ID

The app stores these, plus the date of your first and most recent sign-in, so it can recognise you when you return.

We never see or store your Google password.

## Your resume and job description
- Files you upload are read **in memory** only to calculate your score.
- They are **not saved**, and they are not shared with anyone.
- The optional "AI suggestions" feature, if the site owner enables it, sends the resume text to the Groq API to generate tips.

## How we use your information
Only to let you sign in and use the scorer. We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Storage and deletion
User records are kept in a small database on the hosting server. This database may be reset whenever the app is redeployed.

To ask for your record to be deleted, open an issue at https://github.com/aakashcse/ai-resume-ats/issues. You can also remove the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this page.
